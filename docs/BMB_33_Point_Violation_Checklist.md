# BMB 33-Point Violation Checklist

Based on The Credit Manifesto (Residual Joe) + BMB Standard Operating Procedure.
Use this checklist for EVERY account on a credit report. Check each item — if the data is missing, blank, $0 where it shouldn't be, or contradicts other fields, it's a violation.

---

## How To Use This Checklist

1. For each account, go through all 33 categories below
2. Mark each as: PASS / VIOLATION / N/A (doesn't apply to this account type)
3. Every labeled category on the report MUST have data — blank, dash, or $0 where a real value belongs = INCOMPLETE = violation
4. Cross-check fields against each other (dates vs payment history, status vs balance, etc.)
5. Target: 6-7+ genuine violations per account. If fewer exist, report fewer — NEVER fabricate.

---

## The 33 Categories

### 1. Account Status
- [ ] **What to check:** Is the status correct? (Open, Closed, Charged-Off, Collection, Paid)
- **What it should look like:** Matches the actual current state of the account
- **Red flags:** Status not updated after payment, says "Charged Off" but was settled/paid, says "Open" but account is closed
- **Statute:** FCRA §1681e(b)

### 2. Charge-Off Amount
- [ ] **What to check:** Is the amount that was written off present and accurate?
- **What it should look like:** Dollar amount matching the unpaid balance at time of charge-off
- **Red flags:** BLANK/MISSING (CRITICAL), inflated amount, doesn't match balance at time of charge-off, unexplained amount
- **Statute:** Metro 2 Field 23

### 3. Charge-Off Date
- [ ] **What to check:** Is the date present and correct?
- **What it should look like:** The exact month/year creditor wrote off the account (typically after 6 months delinquency)
- **Red flags:** Missing, future date, doesn't align with 6-month delinquency progression in payment history
- **Statute:** FCRA §1681e(b)

### 4. Original Creditor
- [ ] **What to check:** Is the correct company listed?
- **What it should look like:** Full, correct name of the company that originally extended credit
- **Red flags:** Wrong name, incomplete name, outdated name, missing original creditor on collection accounts
- **Statute:** FCRA §1681g(a)(2)

### 5. Account Number
- [ ] **What to check:** Is the account number present and usable for identification?
- **What it should look like:** Full or partially-masked number (e.g., XXXXXXXXXXXX3651) that allows consumer to identify the account
- **Red flags:** Truncated to point consumer can't identify account, fewer digits than expected, completely missing
- **Statute:** FCRA §1681g(a)(1), Gillespie v. Equifax (CRITICAL)

### 6. Account Type
- [ ] **What to check:** Is the classification correct?
- **What it should look like:** Credit Card, Auto Loan, Mortgage, Installment, Revolving, Collection, Student Loan, etc.
- **Red flags:** Wrong type (affects credit scoring models), credit card listed as installment, mortgage listed as revolving
- **Statute:** Metro 2 Field 12

### 7. Payment History (24-Month Grid)
- [ ] **What to check:** Is the payment grid complete and accurate?
- **What it should look like:** Every month has a status: OK/checkmark (on-time), or 30/60/90/120/150/180 (late). No blank boxes.
- **Red flags:**
  - Blank spaces = NOT 100% maximum possible accuracy (CRITICAL)
  - "ND" (No Data) entries
  - Gaps or missing months
  - Fewer than 24 months shown
  - Delinquency jumps (Current → 120+ without 30→60→90 steps) — Seamans v. Temple University (CRITICAL)
  - Experian commonly has NO data in payment section — flag as incomplete
- **Statute:** FCRA §1681e(b), Metro 2 Fields 18-24

### 8. Last Payment Date
- [ ] **What to check:** Is it present and does it match the payment history grid?
- **What it should look like:** Actual date of last payment, matching the corresponding month in payment grid
- **Red flags:** Blank, "$0" as last payment (TransUnion common — how can last payment be $0?), date says January but January in grid shows something different, last payment date shows a month not even in the grid
- **Statute:** FCRA §1681e(b)

### 9. Date of First Delinquency (DOFD)
- [ ] **What to check:** Is it present? (CRITICAL — determines 7-year removal date)
- **What it should look like:** The exact month/year the account first became delinquent leading to charge-off
- **Red flags:**
  - MISSING = CRITICAL VIOLATION (Experian and TransUnion are notorious for omitting this)
  - DOFD says Feb 2018 but payment history shows payment made that month = contradiction
  - Future date, date after charge-off date
- **Statute:** FCRA §1681c(a)

### 10. Collection Information
- [ ] **What to check:** Is collection agency info accurate and complete?
- **What it should look like:** Current collection agency name, original creditor identified, single listing per debt
- **Red flags:** Outdated agency info, original creditor not identified, same debt listed with multiple agencies (duplicate tradeline)
- **Statute:** FCRA §1681e(b)

### 11. Balance History
- [ ] **What to check:** Does the balance history align with actual account activity?
- **What it should look like:** Logical progression of balances matching payments and charges
- **Red flags:** Incomplete history, unexplained balance jumps, balances not matching credit limit or payment activity
- **Statute:** FCRA §1681e(b)

### 12. Credit Limit
- [ ] **What to check:** Is it present for revolving/credit card accounts?
- **What it should look like:** Dollar amount matching the approved credit limit
- **Red flags:** BLANK/MISSING/$0 for credit cards (affects utilization ratio — VIOLATION), current balance > credit limit without explanation
- **Statute:** Metro 2 Field 16

### 13. Account Opening Date
- [ ] **What to check:** Is the date present, accurate, and consistent?
- **What it should look like:** Exact month/year account was opened; should be same across all three bureaus
- **Red flags:** Blank, invalid date, different dates on different bureaus (which is correct?), opened 11/2015 but payment history shows 60 days late December 2015 (impossible)
- **Statute:** FCRA §1681e(b)

### 14. Responsibility (ECOA Code)
- [ ] **What to check:** Is it correctly designated?
- **What it should look like:** Individual, Joint, or Authorized User — matching the actual account agreement
- **Red flags:** Wrong designation (affects liability), individual listed as joint, authorized user listed as primary
- **Statute:** Metro 2 Field 5

### 15. Dispute History
- [ ] **What to check:** Is there a record of previous disputes?
- **What it should look like:** If previously disputed, should show dispute flag and resolution
- **Red flags:** "Disputed by consumer" comment but NO "In Dispute" flag (CRITICAL), missing dispute history
- **Statute:** FCRA §1681i(a)(5)(A)

### 16. Late Payment Details
- [ ] **What to check:** Are the 30/60/90/120-day late indicators accurate?
- **What it should look like:** Accurate count of late payments by severity, matching payment history grid
- **Red flags:** Late payments without documented verification method, arbitrary late markers, counts don't match grid
- **Statute:** FCRA §1681i(c)

### 17. High Balance / Highest Balance
- [ ] **What to check:** Is it present and accurate?
- **What it should look like:** Highest amount ever owed, matching credit limit or actual peak balance
- **Red flags:** Missing entirely, doesn't match actual account history, higher than credit limit ever was
- **Statute:** FCRA §1681e(b)

### 18. Payment Status
- [ ] **What to check:** Does it match other fields?
- **What it should look like:** Current status accurately reflecting account condition
- **Red flags:** Says "Current" but Past Due > $0, says "Paid" but balance > $0, conflicts with account status field
- **Statute:** FCRA §1681e(b)

### 19. Remarks / Comments
- [ ] **What to check:** Are remarks accurate and not misleading?
- **What it should look like:** Accurate notes reflecting actual account history
- **Red flags:** Misleading remarks, "Settled for less" not noted when settled, incorrect impressions, outdated comments
- **Statute:** FCRA §1681e(b)

### 20. Compliance Condition Codes
- [ ] **What to check:** Are special circumstance codes present when applicable?
- **What it should look like:** Correct codes for natural disaster, active military duty, pandemic forbearance, etc.
- **Red flags:** Missing when applicable, wrong code applied, outdated code
- **Statute:** Metro 2 Compliance Codes

### 21. Original Loan Amount
- [ ] **What to check:** For installment loans — is it present and accurate?
- **What it should look like:** The initial amount borrowed, matching loan documents
- **Red flags:** Missing for installment accounts, incorrect amount, doesn't match loan agreement
- **Statute:** Metro 2 Field 15

### 22. Current Balance
- [ ] **What to check:** Is the amount currently owed accurate?
- **What it should look like:** Actual current balance reflecting all payments and charges
- **Red flags:** Inflated balance, not updated after payment, $0 balance with active charge-off status (conflicting), doesn't match reality
- **Statute:** FCRA §1681e(b)

### 23. Scheduled Payment Amount
- [ ] **What to check:** Is the monthly payment correct?
- **What it should look like:** Amount per original loan agreement; $0 for charged-off accounts
- **Red flags:** Wrong amount, scheduled payment > $0 for charged-off account (should be $0), missing for active account
- **Statute:** Metro 2 Field 17

### 24. Past Due Amount
- [ ] **What to check:** Is it accurate and consistent with status?
- **What it should look like:** Actual amount past due, matching account status
- **Red flags:** Unexplained past due, Past Due > $0 but Status = Current, Past Due = $0 but Status = Charge-off (suspicious)
- **Statute:** FCRA §1681e(b)

### 25. Payment Rating / Status Code
- [ ] **What to check:** Is the numeric status code correct?
- **What it should look like:** Code matching actual account condition (05=Current, 64=Paid Charge-off, 97=Charge-off, etc.)
- **Red flags:** Status 97 for paid account (should be 64), Status 05 but balance > $0, conflicting codes, code not reflecting actual condition
- **Statute:** Metro 2 Field 11

### 26. Narrative Codes
- [ ] **What to check:** Are standardized narrative codes accurate?
- **What it should look like:** Current, accurate codes describing account situation
- **Red flags:** Outdated codes, codes that misrepresent account status
- **Statute:** Metro 2 Standards

### 27. Special Comment Codes
- [ ] **What to check:** Are they accurate and properly explained?
- **What it should look like:** Relevant special comment codes with clear meaning
- **Red flags:** Unexplained codes, conflicting special comments, outdated codes
- **Statute:** Metro 2 Standards

### 28. Estimated Removal Date
- [ ] **What to check:** Is the projected removal date calculated correctly?
- **What it should look like:** 7 years from DOFD (10 years for bankruptcy)
- **Red flags:** Missing, incorrectly calculated (wrong start date), extended beyond legal limit
- **Statute:** FCRA §1681c(a)

### 29. Date Reported
- [ ] **What to check:** Is it recent/current?
- **What it should look like:** Recent date showing the information is being actively maintained
- **Red flags:** Outdated date, inconsistent with other account info, stale reporting
- **Statute:** FCRA §1681e(b)

### 30. Date Updated
- [ ] **What to check:** When was information last updated?
- **What it should look like:** Recent date confirming data freshness
- **Red flags:** Outdated update with current reporting, stale data reported as current
- **Statute:** FCRA §1681e(b)

### 31. Purchased From / Sold To Information
- [ ] **What to check:** For transferred accounts — is transfer chain complete?
- **What it should look like:** Clear chain of custody from original creditor to current holder
- **Red flags:** Missing transfer info, incomplete chain, same debt appearing under both original and purchasing creditor (duplicate)
- **Statute:** FCRA §1681e(b)

### 32. Original Charge-Off Creditor Classification
- [ ] **What to check:** Is the creditor type correct?
- **What it should look like:** Accurate classification (Bank, Credit Union, Finance Company, etc.)
- **Red flags:** Wrong classification, missing classification
- **Statute:** Metro 2 Standards

### 33. Secondary Agency Information
- [ ] **What to check:** For accounts with multiple agencies — is it accurate?
- **What it should look like:** Current, accurate secondary agency details
- **Red flags:** Outdated secondary agency, same debt listed multiple times with different agencies (CRITICAL — duplicate tradeline)
- **Statute:** FCRA §1681e(b)

## SOP Cross-Checks (Do These After the 33 Categories)

### A. Completeness Scan
- [ ] Every labeled category on the report that has NO DATA, a dash, or "$0" where a real value belongs = INCOMPLETE REPORTING

### B. Date Consistency
- [ ] Date Opened should be the same across all three bureaus
- [ ] Last Payment Date should match the payment history grid for that month

### C. Payment Grid Integrity
- [ ] Every box in the payment history should have a value (OK, checkmark, or 30/60/90/120/150/180)
- [ ] No blank boxes anywhere in the grid

### D. Delinquency Progression
- [ ] Late payments should follow logical sequence: 30 → 60 → 90 → 120 → 150 → 180
- [ ] Charge-off typically occurs after 6 months of delinquency
- [ ] No skipping steps (e.g., Current → 120 days is impossible)

### E. Term Period Check
- [ ] Car loans, mortgages, installment loans must have term period listed
- [ ] Term period should be accurate (e.g., 60 months for auto, 360 months for mortgage)

### F. Cross-Field Contradictions
- [ ] If DOFD says Feb 2018, payment history should NOT show a payment that month
- [ ] If status says "Closed," Date Closed should NOT be blank
- [ ] If last payment says March, the March column in the grid should reflect it
- [ ] If Date Opened is 11/2015, there should NOT be a 60-day late in 12/2015

## Bureau-Specific Gotchas

| Bureau | Common Issue | What To Flag |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| **Experian** | No data in payment history section | Blank payment grid = incomplete |
| **Experian** | Missing DOFD | Always check — they often omit it |
| **TransUnion** | "$0" as last payment | How can last payment be $0? Flag it |
| **TransUnion** | Missing DOFD | Always check — they often omit it |
| **Equifax** | Standard categories with missing data | Every category label needs data |

## Quick Reference: Minimum Violations by Account Type

| Account Type | Minimum Expected | Why |
|-------------|-----------------|-----|
| Charge-Off | 5-7+ | Most fields to check, most common errors |
| Collection | 3-5+ | Missing original creditor, DOFD, duplicates |
| Delinquent (Active) | 2-4+ | Payment history gaps, status mismatches |
| Late Payment Only | 2-3+ | Grid errors, progression issues |
