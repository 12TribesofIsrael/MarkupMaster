### Step 2: Check for Common Violations

**A. TRUNCATED ACCOUNT NUMBERS**
- Pattern: Account number showing X's or fewer digits than standard
- Statute: FCRA Â§1681g(a)(1)
- Precedent: *Gillespie v. Equifax*
- Fix: Demand full account number or deletion

**B. MISSING DATE OF FIRST DELINQUENCY (DOFD)**
- Pattern: Charge-off or collection account without DOFD documented
- Statute: Metro 2Â® Field 25; FCRA Â§1681c(a)(4)
- Fix: Demand DOFD documentation or deletion

**C. PAYMENT HISTORY GAPS**
- Pattern: "ND" (No Data) entries in payment history
- Statute: FCRA Â§1681e(b); Metro 2Â® Field 18
- Precedent: *Seamans v. Temple University*
- Fix: Demand complete payment history or deletion

**D. CHARGE-OFF VIOLATIONS**
- Pattern: Status 97 (charge-off) with balance mismatch
- Statute: Metro 2Â® Field 23, 25, 26; FCRA Â§1681e(b)
- Fix: Demand clarification or correction

**E. DELINQUENCY SEQUENCING**
- Pattern: Late payment entries not in 30-60-90-120-150-180 progression
- Statute: FCRA Â§1681e(b); Metro 2Â® Field 18
- Precedent: *Seamans v. Temple University*
- Fix: Demand proper sequencing or deletion

**F. DISPUTE NOTATION WITHOUT "IN DISPUTE" FLAG**
- Pattern: Comment says "disputed by consumer" but no "In Dispute" flag
- Statute: FCRA Â§1681i(a)(5)(A)
- Fix: Demand flag or dispute resolution documentation

**G. DUPLICATE ACCOUNTS**
- Pattern: Same account appears twice with different furnishers
- Statute: FCRA Â§1681e(b); Metro 2Â® FAQ 36
- Fix: Demand deletion of duplicate

**H. AUTHORIZED USER VS. PRIMARY ACCOUNT**
- Pattern: Account marked "Authorized User" when consumer is primary
- Statute: ECOA Â§1691; FCRA Â§1681e(b)
- Fix: Demand responsibility correction

**I. ACCOUNT FRAUD CONCERNS**
- Pattern: Account opened during ID fraud alert period without clear authorization
- Statute: FACT Act; FCRA Â§1681i
- Fix: Demand fraud investigation

**J. PAYMENT TERMS ERRORS**
- Pattern: Credit card showing "1 Month" terms (should be revolving)
- Statute: Metro 2Â® Field 11-12; FCRA Â§1681e(b)
- Fix: Demand terms field correction
