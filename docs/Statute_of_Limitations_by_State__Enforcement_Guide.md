# Statute of Limitations by State: Enforcement Guide

## FCRA DELETION ARGUMENT: SOL EXPIRED = AUTOMATIC DELETION

**Legal Standard:** FCRA §1681c(a) - "In general, except as otherwise provided in this section, no consumer reporting agency shall make any consumer report containing any of the following items of information... any account placed for collection or charged to profit and loss which antedate the report by more than seven years... **or any other derogatory item which antedate the report by more than seven years**."

**Key Principle:** Even if accurate, if SOL has expired, item must be deleted OR dispute is strongest deletion argument available.

## HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE IN DISPUTES

### STEP 1: IDENTIFY DOFD
From credit report, locate "Date of First Delinquency" or equivalent field for each tradeline.

### STEP 2: CALCULATE EXPIRATION DATE
**DOFD + State SOL = Expiration Date**

Example (Delaware):
- DOFD: March 1, 2019
- State: Delaware
- SOL: 3 years
- **Expiration Date: March 1, 2022**

### STEP 3: CHECK TODAY'S DATE
If today's date is **AFTER expiration date**, SOL is EXPIRED.

### STEP 4: INCLUDE IN DISPUTE LETTER
Add to violation description:

> "This account shows a Date of First Delinquency of March 1, 2019. Under [State] law (cite statute), the statute of limitations for this obligation is three (3) years, expiring March 1, 2022. As of this date, the statute of limitations has expired. Pursuant to FCRA §1681c(a), items beyond the applicable statute of limitations period must be deleted from the credit report."

### STEP 5: DEMAND IN LETTER
Include in "Statutory Demands" section:

> "Demand the immediate deletion of [Account Name/Number] based on expired statute of limitations. This item is no longer legally enforceable and must be removed regardless of accuracy."

## SPECIAL RULES & NOTES

### TOLLING
Some states have "tolling" rules that pause SOL:
- **Illinois:** SOL tolls during active litigation
- **New York:** SOL may toll if debtor leaves state
- Verify tolling for your state in dispute letter

### REVIVAL RULES
If creditor obtained judgment, SOL may be extended:
- **Example:** Judgment on March 1, 2022 may create new SOL (10-20 years depending on state)
- Check court dockets for judgments

### CREDIT CARD ACCOUNTS
Most credit cards are "open accounts" rather than written contracts:
- Use "Open Account" SOL for credit cards UNLESS contract says otherwise
- Check original cardholder agreement

### INSTALLMENT LOANS
Installment loans (auto, personal, student) typically use "Installment Loan" SOL:
- Do NOT default to "Written Contract" unless verified
- Review original loan documents

## LITIGATION ADVANTAGE: SOL VIOLATIONS

If CRA refuses to delete expired item:
- **§1681c(a) violation** — statutory duty to remove
- **§1681e(b) violation** — fails to maintain accuracy
- **Willful violation** — if CRA knew or should have known SOL expired
- **Demand:**  Damages under §1681n (willful) or §1681o (negligent)

**Typical damages for willful §1681c violation:** $1,000-$5,000 + attorney's fees
