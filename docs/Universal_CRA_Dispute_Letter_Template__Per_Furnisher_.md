# UNIVERSAL CRA DISPUTE LETTER TEMPLATE
## FCRA §1681i Reinvestigation Demand - Per Furnisher Structure

---

## USAGE INSTRUCTIONS

This is a universal template for generating individual CRA dispute letters.

**Key Points:**
- Generate ONE of these per furnisher with violations
- Replace all [PLACEHOLDERS] with actual data from credit report
- Violation details pulled from violation detection analysis
- Calculate [DEADLINE_DATE] as 30 days from current date
- Each letter contains ONLY violations from that furnisher

## STATUTORY DEMANDS - MANDATORY FCRA §1681i REQUIREMENTS

I demand that you take the following actions within 30 business days per FCRA §1681i:

**A. SUPPRESSION DURING INVESTIGATION** — *FCRA §1681i(a)(5)(A)*

You must suppress (delete from consumer reports) all disputed [FURNISHER_NAME] accounts listed above during your investigation. Under §1681i(a)(5)(A), disputed information must not be reported while you investigate.

**B. FORWARDING TO FURNISHERS** — *FCRA §1681i(a)(2)*

You must forward this complete dispute letter, along with all supporting documentation, to [FURNISHER_NAME] within 5 business days. Current furnisher contact information:

[FURNISHER_NAME]
[FURNISHER_ADDRESS]
Phone: [FURNISHER_PHONE]

**C. METHOD OF VERIFICATION** — *FCRA §1681i(c)*

You must provide me, in writing, with the method by which you verified each disputed [FURNISHER_NAME] account. This must include:
- How you contacted [FURNISHER_NAME]
- What documentation [FURNISHER_NAME] provided to verify accuracy
- Copies of all verification responses
- Specific basis for determining each account is accurate

**D. FULL FILE DISCLOSURE** — *FCRA §1681g(a)*

You must provide a complete copy of my credit file, including all internal investigation notes, communications with furnishers, and dispute handling documentation. This disclosure must include information not reported to third parties.

**E. THIRD-PARTY NOTIFICATION** — *FCRA §1681i(a)(5)(A)*

You must identify all entities to which you have reported the disputed [FURNISHER_NAME] accounts in the past 6 months and notify them that these accounts are disputed. Provide me with a list of these entities.

**F. FURNISHER INVESTIGATION COMPLIANCE** — *FCRA §1681s-2(b)*

Upon receiving this notice, [FURNISHER_NAME] must:
- Conduct reasonable investigation within 30 days
- Review all information I have provided
- Modify or delete inaccurate information
- Report results to me and to you within 30 days
- Inform all CRAs of corrections made

**G. DISPUTE FLAGGING** — *FCRA §1681i(a)(5)(A)*

All disputed [FURNISHER_NAME] accounts must be flagged "In Dispute" on your records. This flag must remain until dispute is resolved or deleted.

**H. NO ADVERSE REINSERTION** — *FCRA §1681i(a)(3)*

If you delete information due to this dispute, you must not re-report it unless [FURNISHER_NAME] certifies in writing that the information is accurate and complete.
