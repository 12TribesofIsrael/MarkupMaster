## CRITICAL ENFORCEMENT POINTS

### DO NOT SKIP ANY STEP
You CANNOT go from Step 2 to Step 8. You MUST complete Steps 3-7 first.

### DO NOT PROCEED WITHOUT SIGN-OFF
Step 7 sign-off is REQUIRED before Step 8. If not signed off, STOP.

### DO NOT GENERATE LETTERS FIRST
The worksheet is SOURCE OF TRUTH. Letters are OUTPUT of worksheet. Never reverse this.

### IF VIOLATIONS FEW
If you find fewer violations than expected (see Step 6 guidelines), STOP and re-check that account using Red Flag Checklist. Likely missed something.

### IF TESTS FAIL
If Step 6 validation fails, go BACK to Step 3/4. Do not proceed to Step 8.

---

## MINIMUM EXPECTED VIOLATIONS

**If you find fewer than these, RE-CHECK:**

| Account Type | Minimum Violations | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Charge-off | 5+ | 33-point audit should identify multiple issues |
| Delinquent | 2-3 | Payment history + status + documentation |
| Collection | 3+ | Original creditor + DOFD + balance issues |
| Late Payment | 2+ | Verification method + date accuracy |
| Disputed (not suppressed) | 2-3 | Suppression failure + investigation failure |
| Open account with problems | 2+ | At minimum: credit limit + payment history |

If you find FEWER than minimums, suspect missed violations.
