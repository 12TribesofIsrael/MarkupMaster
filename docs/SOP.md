Once you have everything circled that is not accurate or complete then you are ready to prepare your dispute letters.
 In the dispute letters to each bureau all the errors you circled or highlighted you need to describe in detail what each issue is and state the items that are either inaccurate or incomplete. Nine times out of ten they will either delete or do nothing but there is a chance they just fix the errors or some of them, so it works in your favor to go through the credit report thoroughly to spot all errors.
Make sure you list each account that you are disputing the 1. account name that is listed on your credit report the 2. account number listed 3.the date opened and 4.the balance.
Attachments should include: 1. Copy of drivers license 2. Copy of social security card 3. copy of utility bill or bank statement 4. Copy of letter describing errors 5. Copy of credit report showing errors
Then you file your CFPB complaint against the credit bureaus after you have mailed everything off and you attach all the mailed documents to your CFPB complaint follow the CFPB guide on how to fill out CFPB complaint and there is also a video attached
Make sure you keep copies of everything you send them, and tracking numbers create a file for yourself or each customer and save all communication between you and the credit bureaus.
