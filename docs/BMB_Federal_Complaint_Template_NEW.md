## COMPLAINT AND DEMAND FOR JURY TRIAL

Plaintiff, by and through self-representation or counsel, alleges as
follows against Defendant(s). This complaint seeks relief under the Fair
Credit Reporting Act, 15 U.S.C. § 1681 et seq., and related law.

### PARTIES

1\. Plaintiff is a natural person and a "consumer" as defined by 15
U.S.C. § 1681a(c).

2\. Defendant is a "consumer reporting agency" and/or a "furnisher of
information" as those terms are used in the FCRA.

### JURISDICTION AND VENUE

3\. Jurisdiction arises under 15 U.S.C. § 1681p and 28 U.S.C. § 1331
(federal question).

4\. Venue is proper in this District because a substantial part of the
events or omissions giving rise to these claims occurred here and/or
Defendant transacts business here.

### FACTUAL ALLEGATIONS

5\. On or about \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_, Plaintiff obtained
and reviewed a consumer disclosure containing inaccurate or incomplete
information.

6\. Plaintiff disputed the inaccurate information with the consumer
reporting agency ("CRA") in writing pursuant to 15 U.S.C. § 1681i.

7\. The CRA failed to conduct a reasonable reinvestigation and/or failed
to delete or correct information that was inaccurate, incomplete, or
unverifiable.

8\. Plaintiff also provided notice of dispute to the furnisher(s). Upon
information and belief, the furnisher(s) failed to reasonably
investigate and to modify, delete, or block the information as required
by 15 U.S.C. § 1681s-2(b).

9\. As a result, Plaintiff suffered damages including but not limited to
credit denials, higher costs of credit, loss of credit opportunities,
emotional distress, and time expended attempting to correct the
inaccuracies.

### CLAIMS FOR RELIEF

COUNT I -- FCRA § 1681i (Failure to Reinvestigate)

10\. Plaintiff realleges the preceding paragraphs. The CRA failed to
conduct a reasonable reinvestigation, failed to review and consider all
relevant information, and failed to delete or correct disputed
information that was inaccurate, incomplete, or unverifiable, in
violation of 15 U.S.C. § 1681i(a).

COUNT II -- FCRA § 1681e(b) (Failure to Assure Maximum Possible
Accuracy)

11\. Plaintiff realleges the preceding paragraphs. The CRA reported
inaccurate information and failed to follow reasonable procedures to
assure maximum possible accuracy, in violation of 15 U.S.C. § 1681e(b).

COUNT III -- FCRA § 1681s-2(b) (Duties of Furnishers Upon Notice of
Dispute)

12\. Plaintiff realleges the preceding paragraphs. After receiving
notice of dispute from a CRA, the furnisher(s) failed to conduct a
reasonable investigation, failed to review all relevant information, and
failed to modify, delete, or block reporting, in violation of 15 U.S.C.
§ 1681s-2(b)(1)(A)-(E).

### DAMAGES

13\. As a direct and proximate result of the foregoing violations,
Plaintiff suffered actual damages including but not limited to denial of
credit, out-of-pocket losses, emotional distress, and time expended. For
willful violations, Plaintiff is entitled to statutory and punitive
damages under 15 U.S.C. § 1681n; for negligent violations, Plaintiff is
entitled to actual damages under 15 U.S.C. § 1681o.

### PRAYER FOR RELIEF

WHEREFORE, Plaintiff respectfully requests that the Court enter judgment
in Plaintiff's favor and against Defendant(s) as follows:

a\. Declare that Defendant(s) violated the FCRA as alleged;

b\. Award actual, statutory, and punitive damages pursuant to 15 U.S.C.
§§ 1681n--1681o;

c\. Award reasonable attorney's fees and costs; and

d\. Grant such other and further relief as the Court deems just and
proper.

### JURY DEMAND

Plaintiff demands trial by jury on all issues triable as of right.
